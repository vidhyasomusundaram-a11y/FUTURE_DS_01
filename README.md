Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard

This repository contains an interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyse E-commerce sales performance. It provides meaningful insights into revenue trends, product performance, and customer purchasing patterns across different regions and years.

1) Tools & Technologies:-
a. Power BI
b. Data modelling (Relationships, Hierarchies)
c. Data Visualisation (Charts, Donut chart, KPI Cards)
d. Slicers (Region & Year Filters)

2) Key Insights & Features:-
a. Sales Trend Over Time- Shows how sales change month-by-month and identifies peak sales months.
b. Sales by Region- Compares performance across West, East, Central, and South.
c. Sales by Category- Insight into Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies contribution to total revenue.
d. Best-Selling Products- Top 5 highest revenue-generating products visualised with monthly sales trends.

3) KPIs
Total Sales: 2.30M
Total Profit: 286.40K
Total Orders: 5009

4) Filters
Region filter for comparative analysis
Year filter for trend visibility over time
